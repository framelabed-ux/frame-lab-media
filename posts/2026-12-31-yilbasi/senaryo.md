### Sahne 1 (referanslı)
A small physical toy robot shaped like a retro computer monitor, exactly like the reference images: matte lime-green plastic body, glossy black screen with a thin lime frame, thin antenna with a glossy magenta ball on top, short lime neck and black stand. It sits on a wooden windowsill at night. Behind it, through a slightly fogged window, heavy snow falls over a quiet city of apartment blocks with warm window lights in soft bokeh. The dark screen flickers on and shows two white dot eyes and a small white smile. Slow macro push-in, tiny dust particles in the warm lamp light. Hyperrealistic, shot on ARRI Alexa, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural film grain, warm interior light against cold blue snowy night. No text, no logos.

### Sahne 2 (referanslı)
Extreme close-up of the toy robot's small glossy black screen, exactly like the reference images. Inside the screen, tiny realistic video moments flip by like a flipbook, about one every half second: steam rising from a tulip-shaped Turkish tea glass in morning light, rain drops on a tram window, a hand writing in a notebook, a dog running on a beach, birthday candles being blown out, a sunset seen from a ferry deck. Faint reflections of falling snow on the screen glass. Locked-off camera. Hyperrealistic, macro lens, natural film grain. No text, no logos.

### Sahne 3
Slow cinematic pull-back through a frosty window at night, revealing a snowy apartment building across the street. In each warmly lit window, anonymous people prepare for New Year's Eve: a grandmother setting a long dinner table, friends laughing around a steaming pan, a young woman on a sofa with a cat, a father lifting a small child to watch the snow. Soft snow falling in the foreground, warm interiors against a cold blue exterior. Hyperrealistic, shot on ARRI Alexa, 50mm lens, gentle and emotional, natural film grain. No text, no logos.

### Sahne 4 (referanslı, sabit kamera)
Locked-off medium close-up of the lime-green toy robot from the reference images, sitting on a wooden windowsill, facing the camera, its screen glowing softly dark green. Outside, snow falls in slow motion. The room is quiet and dim. The window glass begins to reflect faint magenta-pink and lime-green flashes, as if fireworks are about to start. Completely static camera, no camera movement. Hyperrealistic, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural film grain. No text, no logos.

### Sahne 5 (referanslı)
Wide shot over snowy city rooftops at midnight: magenta-pink and lime-green fireworks burst in the sky in slow motion, their light reflecting on the snow-covered roofs. In the foreground, slightly out of focus on the windowsill, the small lime-green toy robot from the reference images, its screen showing a happy smiling face. Slow crane up, glittering sparks, magical and emotional. Hyperrealistic, shot on ARRI Alexa, natural film grain. No text, no logos.
